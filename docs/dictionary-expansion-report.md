## Selection policy

- The original 1,482-word baseline is pinned, so repeated builds are deterministic rather than cumulative.
- `alex1770/wordle` hidden-answer list (MIT) is the main expansion candidate source.
- `tabatkins/wordle-list` (MIT) supplies validity for additional common guesses.
- EJDict (CC0/Public Domain) supplies direct English-to-Japanese glosses and its 2,000-word list alone promotes new entries to EASY.
- `google-10000-english-no-swears` is used only as a non-redistributed frequency/audit signal for candidate priority; it does not by itself make a word an EASY answer.
- JMdict common data and EJDict markers supply POS where they resolve cleanly; POS is intentionally optional rather than guessed.
- Entries without a concise school-safe Japanese gloss are skipped.
- Obvious proper-name, slur, explicit, contraction, and unsuitable candidates are excluded.
- Runtime remains fully local; no source is queried during gameplay.
